Who We Are

Daraja Academy is a fully sponsored, all-girls boarding secondary school located in Naibor, near Nanyuki in Laikipia County, Kenya. Founded in 2009, the school provides free, high-quality education and support to girls from underserved communities.

Vision & Mission

🎯 Our Vision

To empower young women through quality education and mentorship, enabling them to become confident leaders, innovators, and agents of positive change in their communities and beyond.

💼 Our Mission

To provide accessible, world-class secondary education to talented girls from underserved communities, fostering academic excellence, critical thinking, and strong character values.

Our History

The academy officially opened its doors in 2009.

The visionary founders behind this initiative are Jenni and Jason Doherty.
